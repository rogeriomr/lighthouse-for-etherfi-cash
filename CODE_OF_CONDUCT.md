# Code of Conduct

🇧🇷 [Resumo em português](#em-português)

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as its code of conduct. Read the full text there; this page is a summary, and the full text is what applies.

## In short

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Welcome people of every background and level of experience, in issues, pull requests, reviews and any project space.
- **Disagree about ideas, never about people.** Give and take constructive feedback; own your mistakes and learn from them.
- **Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, personal or political attacks, trolling, and publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- **Financial data is private information.** Never post, ask for or repost anyone's transactions, balances, wallet or deposit addresses, card digits or screenshots of their account, including your own in a public thread.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break these rules, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, following the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Covenant.

To report a problem privately, open a [private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) on this repository (it reaches only the maintainers), or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are kept confidential.

## Em português

O projeto adota o **[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/pt-br/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**. Em resumo: trate todo mundo com respeito e boa-fé; discorde de ideias, nunca de pessoas; assédio, insultos, ataques pessoais e expor informação privada de alguém não são aceitos. **Dado financeiro é informação privada:** nunca publique nem peça transações, saldos, endereços de carteira ou de depósito, dígitos de cartão ou prints da conta de ninguém, nem os seus num tópico público. Para relatar algo em particular, abra um *private security advisory* no repositório ou fale direto com quem mantém o projeto.
