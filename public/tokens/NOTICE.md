All files were downloaded on 2026-10-19 and are served from this site. They are never loaded from a third-party CDN.

## Changes made to the files

- **SVG files: cleaned, not redrawn.** Each SVG went through these steps:
  - XML declarations, editor metadata (`data-name`, unused root `id`) and comments were removed.
  - Inline `<style>` rules and `style=""` were converted to the equivalent presentation attributes, so the site's Content-Security-Policy does not affect them.
  - The files were checked for scripts, `foreignObject`, event handlers, external links and embedded raster images. None were found.

  Shapes and colors were not changed.
- **PNG files:** `op.png` and `paxgy.png` were scaled down to 128 × 128 px.
- **`whype.svg`:** the official Hyperliquid mark placed on a circle in the kit's own dark color. See its entry below.
